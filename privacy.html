Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 11, 2026  ·  Invincible Grace Luxury Epoxy & Stain, LLC  ·  Jacksonville, FL

Invincible Grace Luxury Epoxy & Stain, LLC ("Invincible Grace," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and what rights you have regarding your personal information. Please read it carefully before submitting any information through our site.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you submit our free estimate request form. This may include:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address
  • Type of service requested
  • Any additional details you include in your message

We do not collect any information automatically through tracking software, advertising pixels, or analytics tools on this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used solely to respond to your estimate request and communicate with you about your project. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Contact you to schedule a consultation or on-site evaluation
  • Prepare and deliver a customized project quote
  • Answer questions about our services
  • Follow up on your project as appropriate

We will never use your information for unsolicited marketing, and we will never sell, rent, lease, or share your personal information with any third party for their own purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services to deliver core functionality:

  • Web3Forms — Our estimate form transmits your submission to Web3Forms (web3forms.com), which securely forwards it to our email inbox. Web3Forms acts as a message relay only and does not store or use your data for any other purpose. Their privacy policy is available at web3forms.com.
  • Google Maps — Our service area page embeds a map provided by Google. When you view this map, Google may collect certain information in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not receive or control any data Google collects through this embed. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Beyond the above, this website does not load any advertising networks, social media trackers, or external analytics services.

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. Your browser may store basic session data as part of normal web browsing, but we do not set or read cookies ourselves.

5. Data Security

Form submissions are transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking home improvement services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our site, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have submitted to us. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
